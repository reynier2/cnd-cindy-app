⚡ Electrical — Panels & Protection

Let us talk electrical. When I look at a panel I check the amperage — a modern house needs 200 amp service; a 100 amp panel feeding a new addition is an upgrade job right there. I hunt for missing GFCI protection in kitchens, baths and garages because code demands it. I price Southwire 12/2 Romex and I always flag the permit, because unpermitted work kills home sales.

🚿 Plumbing — Pressure & Slope

Plumbing is flow and pressure. I check pressure first — above 80 PSI and code demands a pressure reducing valve or fixtures blow out. Drain slope is a quarter inch per foot: too flat clogs, too steep and water outruns the solids. I price Charlotte Pipe PVC Schedule 40 and I flag the frost line on outside spigots, because a frozen pipe is a burst pipe.

🏠 Siding — Squares & Water Barriers

Siding is a math problem. One square equals 100 square feet of wall. Height times width, subtract windows and doors, add 10 to 15 percent for waste. I price James Hardie fiber cement or CertainTeed vinyl, and I never skip the layers behind it — Tyvek wrap and flashing, because siding is only as good as the water barrier under it.

🛠️ Roofing — Squares & Steepness

Roofing counts in squares too, but I add the steepness factor. A walkable roof is straight labor; a steep roof needs safety gear and more hours, so the price climbs. I price GAF Timberline shingles, synthetic underlayment and drip edge, and I always include tear-off and the dumpster — real money that amateurs forget.

🧱 Concrete — PSI & Curing

Concrete is chemistry. A sidewalk takes 3,000 PSI, a driveway 4,000, and footings want solid bearing — 30 PSI is my soil limit. Water is the enemy: too much in the mix and it cures weak and cracks. That is why I watch cure time and tell you when it is safe to walk and when it hits full strength at 28 days.

🚰 Pipe & Drain — Manning's Equation

Pipe and drain is where I flex engineering. I run Manning's equation — diameter, slope, roughness — to compute flow in CFS and compare it against the city requirement. A 12 inch pipe at 1.5 percent slope moves about 4 CFS; if the city wants 3, you PASS. I stamp PASS or RED FLAG on every run, because a failed inspection costs weeks.

🧱 Masonry — Master Mason Secrets

Masonry is where the real craft lives. First I run the IRA suction test — if brick is bone dry and porous it sucks water out of the mortar and kills the bond. I warn to dampen the pile before laying. Then I check for bleed and creep — if the wall climbs too fast in cold or humid weather the bottom courses sink and heights go off. I watch the crow's foot at corners — if the plumb line drifts off the intersection the lead spirals like a corkscrew. And I track bond patterns — Flemish, English, running bond — row by row, because one brick out of rhythm throws the whole wall.
